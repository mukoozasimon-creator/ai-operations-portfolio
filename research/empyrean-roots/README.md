# Empyrean Roots

Research, community operations and project decisions by Simon Mukooza.

**[Open the final research report (PDF)](Empyrean_Roots_Public_Research_Report.pdf)**

The report covers research across Mbale, Busia, Mukono, Kayunga, Masaka and Jinja; local proxy coordination; multilingual delivery; research tools; planning decisions; and the connection to the PPM tool.

Started January 2023.
